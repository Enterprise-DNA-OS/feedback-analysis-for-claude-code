## Annual bill and limits

$25,000 per year is Thematic's published Foundation price on its [own pricing page](https://getthematic.com/product/plan-and-pricing). The page does not label the currency code, so no USD, NZD or AUD claim or conversion is made. This is a vendor list-price benchmark, not a verified NZ or AU customer's paid invoice. Local customer and CRM snapshot searches returned no Thematic match. No live customer database was accessed. Existing customer use and willingness to switch are unverified.

The vendor [case studies](https://getthematic.com/case-studies) include Vodafone and Watercare. Its [software industry page](https://getthematic.com/industries/software-technology) covers support feedback and product-team analysis. Those establish regional and functional relevance; they do not prove owner-led SME spend.

Scores are editorial judgments, not measured search volumes. Order: annual bill, data-shaped, buyer fit, search demand, one-session buildable, import path.

It already offers natural-language analysis and export tooling.

## Scope and sources

[Response export help](https://help.getthematic.com/article/59-how-to-download-your-data-from-thematic) documents configurable CSV downloads. [API export formats](https://help.getthematic.com/article/105-thematic-api-retrieving-data) define byResponse fields and JSON theme entries. Our importer supports that record shape with aliases and explicit mappings. Every demo record and fixture is fictional; all three page samples are real runs against the demo.

The build covers response records, evidence coding, theme counts, NPS associations, segment comparisons, review queues, actions and retention checks. It is not a trained-model substitute, live survey collector or customer messaging service. Compliance sources and internal-policy limits are in compliance.md.

No further new build is queued. The next run should score another unused family from fresh bill evidence. Existing sister pages and the older building brewery record are not new-build queue items.
